Thinking of working in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait or the UAE? Before you start applying, get your paperwork sorted — it's the single biggest reason applications stall. Here's what Fidelity Connect asks every candidate to prepare.

## 1. A valid Kenyan passport

Minimum six months' validity remaining at the time of travel. If yours is expiring soon, renew at Nyayo House or a Huduma Centre before you apply — this alone can add weeks to your timeline if left last-minute.

## 2. Certificate of Good Conduct

Issued by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI). Apply online via eCitizen; processing typically takes two to three weeks, so start early.

## 3. Medical fitness certificate

Gulf employers require a medical exam from an approved GAMCA-accredited clinic. This checks for communicable diseases and general fitness for the role. Book this once your job offer is confirmed, since results have a validity window.

## 4. Academic and professional certificates

For skilled roles (caregivers, technicians, drivers), bring originals plus copies of relevant certificates. Some professions require KNQA equalization of foreign-recognized qualifications — ask your agency if this applies to your role.

## 5. NITA registration (where applicable)

Certain trades require registration with the National Industrial Training Authority. Your recruitment agency should confirm if your specific role needs this.

## 6. Passport photos and biodata

Standard passport-size photos on a white background, plus a completed biodata form — most agencies will help you prepare this to the destination country's format.

## 7. Signed employment contract

Never travel without a contract that clearly states your job title, salary, working hours, accommodation terms, and contract duration. Read it fully before signing, and keep a copy.

## A note on agencies

Kenya's Ministry of Labour requires recruitment agencies to be licensed. Always confirm your agency's licensing status before paying any fees, and be wary of anyone asking for large upfront payments before you have a signed contract — legitimate agencies are transparent about their process at every stage.
