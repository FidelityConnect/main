**Location:** Nairobi

**Experience Required:** Minimum 2 years

**Application Deadline:** 09/05/2026

Fidelity Connect Ltd is seeking a dynamic, vibrant, and highly creative IT & Social Media Executive to join our team. The ideal candidate will provide IT support across the company while also leading our digital presence through engaging graphics and social media content.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provide daily IT support including troubleshooting hardware, software, and network issues.
- Maintain and monitor company IT systems, equipment, updates, and security protocols.
- Design high-quality graphics for marketing materials, social media posts, and company communications.
- Manage and grow the company’s social media pages (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, X)
- Develop and execute creative social media strategies, calendars, and campaigns.
- Track analytics and trends to improve engagement and brand visibility.
- Collaborate with internal teams to support communication and branding initiatives.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Diploma or Degree in IT, Graphics Design, Digital Marketing, or related field.
- At least 2 years’ experience in IT support, graphic design, and social media management.
- Proficiency in design tools such as Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, or Canva.
- Strong knowledge of social media platforms, trends, and analytics tools.

## Key Competencies

- Strong IT troubleshooting ability.
- Creative and vibrant personality with a passion for content creation.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to multitask, meet deadlines, and work with minimal supervision.
- Attention to detail and strong organizational skills.

## Salary Range

- Ksh 50,000 - 60,000
