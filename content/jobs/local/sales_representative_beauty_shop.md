**Client:** Growing beauty business (Nairobi)

**Application Deadline:** 17/04/2026

## Job Description

- Welcome and assist customers professionally.
- Recommend suitable beauty products based on customer needs.
- Promote and sell skincare, makeup, haircare, and other beauty products.
- Monitor stock levels and support stock-taking.
- Handle customer inquiries and complaints professionally.
- Meet set sales targets and support business growth.

- Certificate or Diploma in Sales, Marketing, Beauty Therapy, or a related field is an added advantage.
- Previous experience in retail sales, preferably in a beauty shop or cosmetics store.

## Skills

- Strong communication and customer service skills.
- Good selling and interpersonal skills.
- Honest, reliable, and well-groomed.
