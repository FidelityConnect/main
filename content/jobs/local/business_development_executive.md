**Client:** Fidelity Connect Ltd

## Job Description

- Identify, develop, and maintain relationships with recruitment partners and clients
- Source new business opportunities and secure recruitment contracts
- Manage client relationships, job orders, and recruitment partnerships
- Coordinate recruitment processes from job demand to candidate selection and deployment
- Handle Saudi recruitment processes, including Musaned and Enjaz, and follow up on related documentation and approvals

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree or diploma in Business Administration, Sales and Marketing, Human Resource Management, or a related field
- 3–5 years of experience in the recruitment industry
- Practical experience with Saudi recruitment systems, particularly Musaned and Enjaz, is mandatory
- Proven track record in business development, client acquisition, and partnership management

## Skills

- Strong negotiation, communication, networking, and relationship management skills
- Ability to work independently, meet targets, and deliver results
